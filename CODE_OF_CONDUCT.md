# Code of conduct

Project discussions should be technical, respectful, and useful to people with different levels of
experience.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks;
- accept correction and give specific, actionable feedback;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate
these rules. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel on the repository host. If no private
channel is available, open a public issue that asks for private contact without naming people or
describing the incident. Maintainers should disclose reported information only to the people needed
to review and resolve the concern.
